<div class="guestbook-poem">
    <div class="guestbook-poem-zh">
        <p class="guestbook-poem-title">《自持》</p>
        <p class="guestbook-poem-body">
            不凭人语定行藏，独抱初心照夜长。<br>
            众口千声风过耳，孤身一念月盈窗。<br>
            良言采撷裁为镜，诤语铭心炼作钢。<br>
            我自岿然还自省，且听且辨且刚强。
        </p>
    </div>
    <div class="guestbook-poem-en">
        <p class="guestbook-poem-title">Steadfast</p>
        <p class="guestbook-poem-body">
            I will not let the crowd decide my way;<br>
            one heart's first vow still lights the long night.<br>
            A thousand voices pass like wind at play;<br>
            one steady thought — and moonlight fills my sight.<br>
            Good counsel gathered, shaped into a mirror;<br>
            sharp reproof engraved in my heart, forged into steel.<br>
            Unmoved I stand, and yet myself I weigh:<br>
            to listen, to discern, and stay unbowed.
        </p>
    </div>
